Read an area's review, and ask about it

Each area can have a literature review, written by the library's agent from its papers. Tap a paper it cites for a peek, or open it at the cited page; the chat beside a review is asked about it.
Review: Evaluating LLMs
A review per area: the kinds of methods, how the field got here, the findings and where papers disagree, each claim linked to its paper and page.
Peek, then open. A cited paper shows its summary first; open it, or open it at the page cited.
Ask the review. The answer checks the papers themselves, and cites their tables.
